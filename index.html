Hold'em Night

No-limit Texas Hold'em · £10 stacks · 10p / 20p blinds · up to 8 players

Table talk

    How it works

    • Pick an empty seat to sit down with £10. Anyone seated can deal once two players are in.
    • Blinds are 10p and 20p. Minimum raise is the size of the last raise.
    • If someone stalls for 45 seconds, anyone at the table can skip them (they check, or fold if facing a bet).
    • Bust? Rebuy for £10 between hands. Leave and come back later and your stack is waiting.
    • The game runs in the host's browser, so the host needs to keep their tab open.
